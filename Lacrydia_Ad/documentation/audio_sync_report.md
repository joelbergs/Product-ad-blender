## 5. Mono compatibility

* Stereo -16.3 LUFS -> mono fold-down -17.8 LUFS (**-1.5 dB**; < 3 dB = no phase-cancellation problem).
* L/R correlation overall 0.42, worst second 0.22 (never negative); side/mid energy -3.9 dB.
* Every individual asset is also audited (`asset_audit` in the metrics JSON): clipping, DC, first/last sample, mono loss, L/R correlation. Sub-bass partials are phase-locked L=R (an earlier version used drifting L/R detune on sub sines and measured -11 dB mono loss - fixed).

## 10. What is verified, and what is not

**Verified numerically / visually:** exact length (1,440,000 samples), -16 LUFS integrated, true peak, no clipping, DC, near-silent opening / natural decay / zero at the end (no tail clicks), absence of long silent gaps, mono fold-down, frame- and sample-exact placement of every sound against the config events (Blender mixdown == plan), a/v onset alignment of the muxed test file, sound-by-sound presence at the right time and frequency band on the spectrogram, deterministic regeneration (identical MD5s on re-synthesis).

**Needs human listening (not verifiable here):** timbre quality and taste of every sound; whether whooshes are 'felt' rather than 'heard' on the final playback system; balance of the lens glint / screen bloom / logo against the score on headphones, laptop and phone speakers; whether the score feels premium rather than generic; perceived width and mono fallback beyond the numeric checks; AAC artefacts on quiet material; real-time playback inside Blender.
